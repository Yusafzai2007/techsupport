About Tech Support Hub

Tech Support Hub was founded in the year 2024 as a platform to assist students in dealing with various daily problems that arise in computers as well as software. With time, the platform successfully served over 200 clients as a professional service provider.

Our team consists of motivated students in the field of computer studies, along with professionals who are highly committed to providing effective solutions to various technological challenges.
