Our Services

We offer comprehensive tech support solutions for individuals and small businesses. Our services include computer troubleshooting, software installation, virus removal, data recovery, and network setup. Each service is delivered by certified technicians with minimum turnaround time. We also provide remote assistance for quick fixes and on-site support for complex hardware issues.

About Tech Support Hub

Founded in 2024, Tech Support Hub began as a student initiative to help peers with basic computer issues. Today, we serve over 200 clients with professional technical support. Our team consists of IT students and certified professionals passionate about solving technology challenges. We believe in affordable, transparent, and effective tech solutions for everyone.
